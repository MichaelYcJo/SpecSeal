## What this phase found

**The criterion had to go in beside the lists, not only the lists.** The
failure scenario `plan.md` names for the chosen approach is the refused list
growing by analogy — somebody meets a fourth form, reads the list as a promise
that the gate catches such things, and adds a case. Two or three of those and
the narrow refusal is the general sanitiser #402 refuses. The defence is that
the two halves of the criterion are written in the same section, so the next
reader meets the argument rather than an enumeration. A case pins that they
are there.

**A block quote breaks `flat()` in a way worth recording.** The criterion is a
two-line quote, and folding whitespace without dropping the `> ` marker leaves
`the exit code the > gate reads` — a phrase nobody wrote and no case can
assert. The module's `flat()` strips quote markers before folding. Any later
document case that pins a quoted sentence needs the same.

**The pipe's entry carries its measurement rather than the spec's promise.**
Phase 1 found that a pipe cannot reach the row (its record holds the
measurement). The allowed list says so in the same cell that says a pipe is
legal, because a template that promises a form nobody can write is the shape
this work item exists to end.

**Every one of the seven cases was seen red by deleting the sentence it
pins**, one at a time, restored from bytes kept in the driver rather than from
HEAD. None stayed green while broken.
