# Survivors — the fold ships, and the corpus is still on disk

## Whole ranges

| Range | Grounds |
|---|---|
| `origin/release/v0.13.1...HEAD` | **This branch removes 88 released work items' directories whole, and every sentence of them standing elsewhere is the fold working rather than a defect.** `skills/settle/SKILL.md` §*What a fold branch owes* names this branch shape by name and ships the range row for it: "a fold removes shipped sections whole, and their sentences stand in the durable copies by design". The durable copies are what the fold is **for** — a work item's `spec.md` is absorbed into a `docs/` policy document before its directory is removed, so the folded sentence surviving in `docs/` is the mechanism, not a leftover. The rest are records of what a release did: `CHANGELOG.md`'s gathered entries, `seal/ledger.md`'s folded sections, and the eleven work item directories this branch keeps. **Not one of them may be corrected.** A released changelog entry and a folded ledger row are records of a past release, and rewriting them to match today's policy destroys the thing they are kept for; a `docs/` sentence carrying a `<!-- specs/<id> -->` marker is the fold record itself, and correcting it away would leave `settle --retire` refusing the directory it already removed. Writing one row per surviving sentence is not an escape anybody takes at this scale — the fourth occurrence of this range shape, after #293's 153, #328's 34 and #345's 23, and the largest by an order of magnitude. This row is anchored on the range **and** on this work item, so it stops holding the moment the check runs over a different range. |
