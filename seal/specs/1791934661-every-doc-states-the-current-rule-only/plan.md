## Summary

Each `docs/` file is partitioned rule by rule — contract, which stays in the doc with `Enforced by:`, or mechanics, which moves to the first sentence of its test's docstring or to the code's — and then rewritten to the contract alone. The three files at the ceiling go first; `docs/the-evidence-ledger.md` is deleted and its still-ruling statements moved; phase 10 brings the five files phases 1, 6 and 8 already compacted under the criterion in one pass, so their pins move once more and not twice. Phase 1's file, measured section by section, keeps ≈ 265 of 501 lines (`spec.md` §*The problem, measured*); every other bar is derived from that partition and re-measured by its phase. No ledger row, survivor row, script or checker is written: #944 retires the readers, `bin/rules` (#944 phase 1) prints the whole, and the check and the harness are #923's.
