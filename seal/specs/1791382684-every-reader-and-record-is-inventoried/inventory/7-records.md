### Observations

- **The freeze turned re-reads into rows.** `Ledger frozen from` arrived with #736 (2b1dcb1f, inside v0.18.0). `Re-read ·` rows went from 0 at v0.17.0 to 67 at v0.18.0 and 779 now, and 84% of the ledger rows landed since v0.18.0 are re-reads or corrections (footnote [d], per-file split above). The ledger's coordinates grew from 4,630 to 8,247 (+78%) and its release bytes from 2.65 to 3.56 MB. A row leaves only with its code, and a `Corrected ·` row never leaves (`docs/the-evidence-ledger.md` §*A released row is read again*).
- **A re-read re-triggers itself.** A `Re-read ·` row is anchored on the same unit it re-read (`evidence_check.py:family_view` keys on the coordinate), so the next edit to that unit drifts the family again and owes another row. The units cited most by the bookkeeping rows of 0.18.1–0.20.0 are `evidence_check.py#reverify` 84, `#main` 44, `templates/config.md#"## Broad gate"` 42, `broad_gate.py#gate` 39, `docs/the-evidence-ledger.md#"## A released row is read again…"` 31 and `survivor_check.py#corrected` 30. These are whole-unit anchors on the largest units and sections, so almost any edit drifts them.
- **The loop #834 draws, with numbers.** Rule 3 of `templates/config.md` went 67 → 338 → 654 → 1,345 → 1,211 words across five commits (edee5ca2, b3319a0f, db250692, 6de64c19, ec09faf9), one per broad-gate redesign. 57 ledger coordinates anchor its whole `## Broad gate` section, so each edit owes a re-read of all of them. #825's redesign (6de64c19, PR #846) then carried 40 `Re-read ·`, 11 `Corrected ·` and 147 `survivors.md` rows, and `seal/releases/0.20.0.md` holds 51 corrections of earlier releases' claims.
- **The re-read tool keeps rewriting its own records.** The machinery that writes and judges these rows is `--reverify`, `family_view` and `reverify_into`. Since v0.18.0, 12 issues are about it: #772, #774, #781, #785, #791, #792, #806, #808, #809, #810, #824 and #833. Its own fix PRs carry the most bookkeeping: #829 (#824) has 49 re-reads, 18 corrections and 87 survivors; #801 has 104 re-reads and 96 in-place rewrites before its squash; #786 has 95 and 87.

Five `--retire-process` commits then deleted 129,448 lines. Before the squash (0.18.1–0.18.3), round records were 25,566 of 78,552 added lines.
