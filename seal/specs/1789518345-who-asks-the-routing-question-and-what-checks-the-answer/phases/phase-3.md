## What this phase found

**`agents/framer.md` §*The three writes* said `not routing.md` in so many
words.** §6 makes a definition's named writes the whole of its agent's
permission, so the act did not merely move — a prohibition had to be lifted in
the same edit. The section is now §*The four writes*, `routing.md` is the
first row of its table, and the phrase is gone from the "Nothing else" list.
A reader who finds the act granted in one section and refused in another
follows whichever they reach first, which is the failure this whole work item
is about one level up.

**A prose-count guard fired on a sentence that has nothing to do with the
rung.** `test_the_top_rung_names_behaviour_rather_than_a_count` sweeps
`skills/implement/SKILL.md` for file counts, and the new `routing.md` row said
*before its own three files* — the framer's own writes, not a threshold. The
allow-list in that module is keyed by file and extending it is a conscious
act, so the sentence was reworded instead: *before the framer's other writes*.
Extending an allow-list to admit a phrase that is not the thing being guarded
is how the guard stops meaning anything.

**The frame's own `Framed` line cannot be written by the party it names**, and
`plan.md` rule 2 says so: `agents/framer.md` is read at spawn time, so this
edit cannot reach the session that wrote these documents. This work item's
mark is therefore `smith`'s write, naming a framer that did frame it. It is
the one instance where the party writing the mark is not the party it names,
and it lands in phase 7 with the record in `overview.md`. Every work item
after this one has the framer write it.

**`templates/sdd-spec.md` had no foot-line convention and now shares one with
two other files.** The case asserts the mark is the LAST line of the template
rather than merely present, because a mark in the middle of a document is a
mark the check at the pull request would have to hunt for, and `routing.md`
and `plan.md` both end with their line.

**The work item's own `overview.md` was opened here rather than at phase 7.**
`test_every_spec_directory_that_reached_the_ladder_has_an_overview` was red
for this directory from the branch's first commit — a `spec.md` and a
`plan.md` with no memo beside them — and the skill says to open the memo at
the first divergence. Two had already happened by phase 2, so the red and the
rule pointed at the same moment. It carries both divergences, the two
unverified items with their answerer, and the fed-back clauses.
