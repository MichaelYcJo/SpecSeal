### Changed

- **The terminal seal is the owner's hand-drawn § on a 14-cell disc that
  stands inside the sheet (#832).** The disc used to be a 24-cell lily, or
  for one phase a 24-cell § averaged by area, pressed over the sheet's
  lower-right corner with its edge blended into the parchment. The owner
  compared renders in their own terminal and chose a smaller disc drawn in
  four flat colours: the ring, the field, the § and its one-cell shadow,
  with no blend and no highlight. The disc is 14 cells across and 7 lines
  tall at every scale, because a chart drawn by hand has one size. The
  sheet keeps its height and widens to the right until no text stands under
  the disc, then three columns more. The letter twin writes four letters
  for the disc: `m` the ring, `.` the field, `Y` the § and `y` its shadow.

- **Two real runs' seals share one `Stop` message again.** A real run's
  stamp is about 3,000 UTF-16 units now, against 7,180 for the 24-cell
  disc, so the hook carries two in one message and leaves a third for the
  next turn. The hook's ladder keeps one rung, 0.90, and a seal too large
  for its disc alone is still drawn as the sheet with no disc.
