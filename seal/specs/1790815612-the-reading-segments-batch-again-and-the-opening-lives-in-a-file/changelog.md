- **The framer's opening lives in its definition, and it bounds the opening
  call instead of forbidding batched reads (issue #640).** Since 0.12.3 every
  framer spawn was told, in its prompt alone, not to open with a parallel read
  burst, and every framer reading since sat at 1.00–1.12 tools per turn; the
  0.12.2 framers, never told, read 1.48–1.64. The rule was in no file, which
  is the state #107 exists to end. `agents/framer.md` now carries a section of
  its own: write a skeleton `spec.md` inside the first few calls, so a spawn
  the no-progress watchdog takes leaves something behind; keep one call to
  about six reads or ranges and never the whole reading list; read a large
  file by range. Nothing in it forbids reads going out together, and the
  definition gains the framer's own §10 number, 1.4 tools per turn, under the
  1.46–1.79 band the framers of 0.11.2–0.12.2 read.
