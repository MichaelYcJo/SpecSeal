7. **What the guard swallows.** `except OSError` around a write catches `PermissionError` and also `IsADirectoryError`, `OSError [Errno 28] No space left`, and a read-only filesystem. Ask whether every one of those deserves the same sentence, and whether any of them means something the operator would be misled by. The sentence names permissions in its own wording via `strerror`; check what it prints when `strerror` is `None`, which the branch says it handled.
8. **What phase 1 changed that phase 2 measured differently from the handoff.** The orchestrator's handoff said phase 1 touched two of R2's anchors; the branch reports it touched one, and that the six case anchors did not move because an anchor is a unit and a function added beside them does not move them.
